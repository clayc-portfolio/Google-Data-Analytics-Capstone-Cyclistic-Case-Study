# Google-Data-Analytics-Capstone-Cyclistic-Case-Study
Cyclistic Case Study 1
# Introduction
Cyclistic is a bike sharing program based in Chicago Illinois. They have more than 5,800 bikes with over 600 docking stations. Bikes can be rented from one station and returned at another location. Riders can use standard bikes, as well as tricycles, cargo bike3s, and reclining bikes which make them accessible for many people. 

Riders are broken down into two groups, members and casual riders. About 30% of riders are members who are commuting to work each day. Financial analysis shows that memberships are a key player to the success of Cyclistic.
